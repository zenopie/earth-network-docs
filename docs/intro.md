# Earth Network

On most blockchains, whoever holds the most tokens decides everything. Earth
splits the power in two. Half of everything the chain issues is steered by
people, one vote each, and half by capital, in proportion to stake. Every change
to the rules needs both to agree.

Being a person here is proved with your passport. Your phone reads its chip and
builds a zero-knowledge proof that a government signed it, and the chain checks
the proof. Your name, date of birth and passport number never leave the phone.
One passport is one registration, however many wallets you own.

## Four pillars, fixed for good

Earth issues 4 ERTH a second, for ever, in four equal parts of 1 ERTH a second.
Two are decided by capital and two by people. In each pair, one pays individuals
directly by rule, and the other is a shared fund that voters point somewhere.

| | Paid to individuals, by rule | A shared fund, pointed by vote |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Capital decides** | **Staking rewards**, paid in proportion to bonded ERTH | **Groundworks fund**, steered by stake |
| **People decide** | **ANML buyback**: the chain buys and burns the ANML that registered humans claim | **Caretaker fund**, one vote per human |

The split is written into the software as a constant. No proposal can move money
from one pillar to another, because any split a vote could change would be won
by whichever side was strongest that year. Changing it means shipping a new
chain.

The two kinds of weight cannot be converted into each other. Buying ERTH gets you
no vote in the Caretaker fund, and a passport gets you no weight in the
Groundworks fund. To steer all of the chain's spending you would have to capture
both.

## Two tokens

**ERTH** is the currency. It pays fees, secures the chain through staking, and is
what all four pillars pay out.

**ANML** is the personhood dividend. Each registered human can claim **1 ANML a
day**. A quarter of all issuance buys ANML back and burns it, so the chain is a
permanent buyer of what humans claim.

## Two houses

Every governance proposal needs a **stake house**, where votes are weighted by
bonded ERTH, and an **assembly**, where each registered human has one vote. A
proposal that no human votes on fails. See [Governance](./governance.md).

## No insider allocation

There are no founder tokens, no team unlock and no investor tranche. The genesis
supply went to liquidity and to the reward for registering. After genesis, ERTH
is earned: by registering, staking, providing liquidity, or being voted a share of
a fund. See [Emission](./emission.md) for the exact split, including the small
amounts that genesis did give to individual accounts.
