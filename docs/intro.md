# Earth Network

Earth is a blockchain with two rules that define it:

- One human is one account. A passport and a zero-knowledge proof prove this.
- Issuance is divided equally between people and capital.

Most chains give influence in proportion to holdings. Earth pays half of its
issuance to registered humans, at one share each. It pays the other half to
capital. The two streams are separate. Neither side can outvote the other.

## The two tokens

**ERTH** is the network currency. It pays fees. It secures the chain through
staking. The four emission streams pay out in ERTH.

**ANML** shows that you are a registered human. Each registered person can claim
**1 ANML per day**. Nobody can claim two times, because registration is bound to
a passport and not to a wallet.

## No allocation

There are no founder tokens, no team unlock, and no investor tranche. You must
earn all ERTH after the genesis pre-mine. There are four methods: registration,
staking, liquidity provision, and a vote of a fund share.

The pre-mine went to liquidity. It did not go to persons. See
[Emission](./emission.md).
